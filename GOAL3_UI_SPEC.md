# GOAL3_UI_SPEC.md

## Workbench
新增 Managed Work Queue、SLO status、Delivery lifecycle、Human fallback、Acceptance state、Baseline vs candidate comparison。

## Evolution Center
显示 Candidate type、Evidence window、Baseline metrics、Expected benefit、Risk、Evaluation status、Shadow status、Certification status、Promotion readiness、Rollback，并增加 Deterministic Distillation Opportunities 与 Replacement Opportunities。

## Console
新增 Certification Runs、Capability Releases、Managed Deliveries、Delivery failures/retries、Replacement Records、Capability suspension/deprecation、rollback。

## Hub
新增 CertifiedWorkCapability、CapabilityRelease、CertificationEvidence、CompatibilityProfile、EvaluationHistory、DeploymentProfile。

## Replacement Compare
同屏比较 Existing/Manual、Morn Orchestrated、Morn-native Candidate。不要只显示综合分数，必须展开 quality/time/human load/cost/failure/policy/outcome。
